Scientists spend time sorting papers before they can assess the evidence.

Review time, missed relevant papers, and the effort needed to correct the output.

Scientists review the evidence. Generated summaries are not treated as verified findings.

A FEW PRACTICAL QUESTIONS

Before we talk.

Is this right for our team?

This is a good fit for biotech teams with a specific workflow bottleneck, an AI idea to evaluate, or a build-vs-buy decision to make. You don’t need a finished brief—just a problem and someone who understands the current process.

Do we need to know exactly what to build?

No. The assessment is designed to clarify that. We can start with the task that takes too much time or the decision your team is struggling to make.

How do scope and pricing work?

We’ll discuss the problem first, then agree on deliverables, timing, and fees before an engagement begins. An initial assessment doesn’t commit you to a larger implementation.

What about confidential or sensitive data?

Keep your initial inquiry high level. Before project data is shared, we’ll agree on confidentiality, access, permitted tools, and any security requirements. A pilot should be designed around those constraints from the start.
